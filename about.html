Modern Era Plus Sdn. Bhd. is a Malaysian-owned company based in Miri, Sarawak, established in 2012.

Our company works with industrial facilities and project teams on engineering, construction, fabrication, maintenance, living-quarter refurbishment and technical services.

The original corporate profile highlights an emphasis on timely completion, effective problem-solving and the adoption of health, safety, security and environmental practices.
